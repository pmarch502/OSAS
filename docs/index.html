Once Saved, Always Saved (OSAS) Assessment

Passage-by-passage evaluation of all 260 NT chapters on whether the text addresses the permanence or conditionality of a believer's standing before God. Built on neutral first-century readings with no theological framework applied.

Methodology

Every chapter of the New Testament receives a neutral first-century textual reading: what does the text say, and what would its original audience have heard? These readings form a shared foundation. Topic-specific assessments then evaluate the readings through a particular lens without re-reading or reinterpreting the source text.

All analysis is produced by Claude Opus 4.6 (Anthropic). The project author reviews the output for factual errors and overlooked textual evidence. Where a problem is identified, the passage is returned to Claude with the specific textual question — the human identifies what to re-examine; the AI produces the revised reading. The correction prompt and all original prompts are published in the repository.
